Delete your Parking Tracker account

App: Parking Tracker (Android) · Developer: [Christien C. Canda] · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Parking Tracker and log in.
  2. Tap the address bar at the top, then Settings.
  3. Tap your name at the top of Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted right away. You can tick “Also erase my spots, car, history and settings on this phone” to remove the data stored on your phone too.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email [christiencanda0@gmail.com] with the subject “Delete my Parking Tracker account”. Send it from the email address you used to sign up, so we can confirm it is you. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, username and login from our server.
  • Your login session. You will be logged out.

What is not deleted

  • Anonymous parking reports (“full” or “available”). They are not linked to your account or name, so they cannot be tied back to you.
  • Data stored only on your phone (saved spots, car, history, settings). It is removed when you uninstall the app or choose to erase it while deleting your account.
