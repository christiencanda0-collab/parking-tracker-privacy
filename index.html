Privacy Policy

Parking Tracker (Android app) · Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Parking Tracker uses your location to find parking near you and to give directions. You need an account (email, username and password) to use the app. Your saved spots, car location and history stay on your phone. We do not sell your data, and the app has no ads and no analytics. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Parking Tracker is developed by [Christien C. Canda] (“we”, “us”). The app helps drivers find parking within walking distance of where they are or where they are going.

Contact: [christiencanda0@gmail.com]

2. Information we collect

InformationWhyWhere it goes
Account details: email address, username, and your password. To create your account and log you in. Supabase Authentication. Your password is stored only as a secure hash and is never kept in the app. We cannot read it.
Precise location (GPS), only while you use the app. To find parking near you, show routes and give voice directions. Used on your phone. The coordinates needed for a request are sent to the map services in section 4 (for example, to look up nearby parking or a route). We do not keep a location history on our servers.
Parking reports (optional): the parking’s ID, “full” or “available”, the parking’s location, a random device code and the time. To tell other drivers if a parking is full or available. Our Supabase database. These reports are only sent if you turn on “Share my reports”. They do not include your name or email.
Data kept only on your phone: saved spots, car location and notes, parking history, favorites, ratings, places such as Home and Work, settings and your login session. So the app works and remembers your choices. Stays on your phone. We do not receive it. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not collect your contacts, photos or files.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app: log you in, find and rank parking, show routes and give directions.
  • To show recent “full” or “available” reports from other drivers.
  • To protect the service from misuse (for example, limiting how often reports can be sent).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Services that receive data

To work, the app talks to these services. They receive the information needed for each request, such as map coordinates and your IP address.

  • Supabase (accounts and parking reports). Privacy policy
  • OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, place search and addresses, nearby parking data, and walking and driving routes). Privacy policy
  • Open-Meteo (rain forecast for a location). Terms and privacy
  • Google Maps only if you tap “Open in Google Maps”. This opens the Google Maps app or website with your destination.

Voice directions use your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine.

5. Where your data is stored

Account and report data are stored in a Supabase project hosted in Sydney, Australia. By using the app you understand that this data is processed outside your country.

6. How long we keep data, and deletion

  • Account: kept until you delete it. You can delete it in the app (Settings, then tap your name, then “Delete account”), or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting removes your email, username and login from our server.
  • Parking reports: they are anonymous and not linked to your account, so they stay after you delete your account. They are only used to show recent parking availability.
  • Data on your phone: removed when you uninstall the app, or when you choose “Also erase my spots, car, history and settings on this phone” while deleting your account.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and you can object to or limit how we use it. If you are in the Philippines, these rights come from the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). To make a request, email [christiencanda0@gmail.com] from the address you signed up with. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as hashes. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Parking Tracker is meant for drivers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
